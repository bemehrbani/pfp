FOLDER GUIDE
------------
1. 01_For_Light_Background/
   Use on white, cream, or light surfaces (print documents, white slides, light websites).
   - Includes Vector SVG, Vector PDF, High-Res Transparent PNG, and White-Card PNG.

2. 02_For_Dark_Background/
   Use on dark navy, black, or dark photo surfaces.
   - Note: Transparent PNGs in this folder use light ivory (#F5F3EF) text/dove, so they
     appear pale if previewed on a white window; place on a dark background or view the
     "_On_Dark_Navy_Card.png" files.

3. 03_Single_Color_Laser_Engraving/
   100% solid single-color (#000000 Black & #FFFFFF White) closed-path vectors (SVG & PDF)
   with zero strokes, zero gradients, and zero font dependencies — ready for CNC / CO2 /
   fiber laser engraving on crystal, glass, brass, acrylic, wood, or stone plaques.
