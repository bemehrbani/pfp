## 🎨 Part 2: Art Showcase & Physical Memorial Installation

To transform the venue into an immersive cultural and human rights space:

1. **"100 Faces of Peace" Portrait Gallery:**
   * 12–16 high-resolution mounted boards featuring artistic portraits of the young students and female educators.
   * Minimalist typography in Finnish and English with names, ages, and brief biographical notes.
2. **Symbolic Memory Installation (Centerpiece):**
   * One vintage wooden school desk placed under a warm spotlight.
   * Symbolic artifacts: A burned textbook, a child's backpack, pencils, and **156 white tea-light candles** honoring each certified martyr.
3. **Soundscape & Acoustic Atmosphere:**
   * Gentle coastal Persian Gulf ambient melodies (traditional Nay-anban and orchestral strings) playing during reception and exit.

### 📋 Core Panel Themes for Sama Barvari:

1. **Chapter 1: The Factual & Forensic Baseline**
   * Breakdown of the strike on Shajareh Tayyebeh Primary School (`27°08'43"N 57°05'02"E`).
   * The **156 certified martyrs** (120 elementary pupils, 26 educators/staff, 7 parents, 2 community workers, 1 unborn infant) and **95+ injured**.
   * Physical munitions proof: Recovered BGM-109 Tomahawk cruise missile booster and wing-actuator fragments.
2. **Chapter 2: The Duty of Precaution & Intelligence Negligence**
   * Deconstructing the "outdated 2013 mapping data" defense under Additional Protocol I to the Geneva Conventions (Article 57 - Precaution in Attack).
   * Satellite proof from 2016 showing concrete wall separation and continuous 10-year civilian educational use.
3. **Chapter 3: Legal Avenues in Finland & Europe**
   * Universal Jurisdiction mechanisms under Chapter 1, Section 7 of the Finnish Criminal Code.
   * Submission to the International Criminal Court (ICC) Office of the Prosecutor under Article 15 of the Rome Statute.
   * Nordic parliamentary engagement and victim compensation trusts.
