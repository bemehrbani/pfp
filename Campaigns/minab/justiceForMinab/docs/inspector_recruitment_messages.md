# Messages for Operations Inspector Recruitment

## Message for Jussi

---

Hey Jussi,

I'm setting up a Finnish registered association (yhdistys / ry) called **People for Peace & Justice**. I'd love to ask you something small but meaningful.

**The backstory:** On February 28 — the first day of the US-Iran war — a US airstrike hit the Shajareh Tayyebeh Girls' Elementary School in Minab, Iran. 168 people were killed. Most of them were schoolgirls, aged 7 to 12. The US military's own investigation found they were "likely responsible" — the school was misidentified as a military target because of outdated intelligence data. These weren't statistics. They were kids sitting in classrooms.

We've identified and verified 100 of the children by name and photo. You can see them here: **https://peopleforpeace.live/memorial.html**

I'm building an association to pursue legal accountability — through the ICC, universal jurisdiction, and UN mechanisms. We have three founders (me, and two board members from the Netherlands) and we're filing registration with PRH shortly.

Finnish law requires that every association has an **operations inspector (toiminnantarkastaja)** who is independent from the board. I'd love to ask if you'd be willing to take on this role.

**What it actually means in practice:**
- Once a year, you review the association's annual accounts and write a short report (1 page) confirming the finances are in order
- That's literally it — one task per year
- You don't need to be an accountant or auditor — it's a standard light-touch role in Finnish associations
- You're not on the board, not liable for decisions, and have no financial obligations
- The first year we'll have essentially €0 in transactions (registration fee + maybe small donations), so the initial review would be trivial

**Time commitment:** ~2-3 hours per year maximum.

Would you be up for it? Happy to explain more if you have questions.

Kiitos! 🙏
Mahdi
