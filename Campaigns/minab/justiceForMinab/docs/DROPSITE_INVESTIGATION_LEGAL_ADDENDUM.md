## 3. PRIMARY EVIDENCE INGESTION BY EXHIBIT SERIES

### A. EXHIBIT SERIES WIT: ACTIVATION OF WITNESS DEPOSITIONS

Until this production, Exhibit Series `WIT` was formally preserved under Rule 54bis pending unredacted witness production. The Drop Site News investigation directly provides named, verifiable on-the-record eyewitness, first-responder, and expert medical testimony:

#### 1. Activation of `EXH-WIT-007.1`: Surviving Faculty & Staff Testimonies
* **Witness:** **Hossniyeh Salem** (Preschool Teacher, Shajareh Tayyebeh School; 7-year faculty tenure).
* **Eyewitness Testimony Summary:**
  * Arrived at 7:00 AM with her 10-year-old son Yassin (a 5th-grade pupil at the boys' section).
  * School layout: Lower floor boys' school, upper floor girls' school, dedicated preschool wing in same structure; civilian pupils from all backgrounds, including working-class families and educators.
  * Timeline of Attack: At ~9:30 AM, received notification of regional airstrikes; began calling parents to evacuate pupils calmly.
  * First Impact: Nearby detonation blew out windows, spraying glass. She sheltered 5-year-old preschoolers under desks.
  * Direct Strike Sequence: Described hearing two simultaneous incoming missile sounds: *"Wooosh, boom! Woosh, boom! Plaster falling, ground shaking..."*
  * Evacuation: Brought barefoot preschool children out through smoke and dust, yelling to incoming rescue teams: *"Run to the school—they hit the school! The children are under the ruins. Go save them!"*
  * Cross-Examination Utility: Rebuts any claim of military occupation, armed personnel, or weapons storage on campus.

#### 2. Activation of `EXH-WIT-007.2`: First Responders, Fire & Emergency Medical Leadership
* **Witness 1: Mehran Zakeri** (Chief of the Minab Fire Department):
  * Arrived within minutes of initial strike; reported catastrophic structural pancake collapse.
  * Verified horrific biological casualty conditions: *"When we removed the remains of the martyrs, we had pieces of these dear martyred students' brains under our fingernails... The next day I had to cut my fingernails because the brain matter couldn't easily be removed."*
  * Institutional History: Confirmed campus had ceased any military role 13–14 years prior, functioning purely as an elementary school, medical clinic, car wash, and commercial zone.
* **Witness 2: Majid Dadania** (Iranian Red Crescent Emergency Response Team):
  * Schoolyard was carpeted in severed body parts of children.
  * Documented the second wave of strikes at 1:00–2:00 PM: four missiles struck the Shahid Absalan Clinic, co-op, and car wash 50–80 meters away while crowds of rescuers were digging out children.
  * Photographed the bereaved father speaking to his child's recovered schoolbag and shoes.
* **Witness 3: Ehsan Mohseni** (Minab Coordinator, Iranian Red Crescent Society; 15-year veteran):
  * Located less than 1 km from the school; recorded three missile strikes in approximately 53–54 seconds.
  * Observed children's bodies thrown into surrounding trees and pulverized against masonry.
* **Witness 4: Dr. Mohammad Movarekh** (Director, Hazrat Abolfazl / Minab General Hospital):
  * General hospital capacity overwhelmed within 60 minutes; morgue has only 6 cooling drawers.
  * Hospital converted kitchen and cafeteria cooling facilities to preserve remains; requisitioned commercial freezer trucks from local coastal fish and shrimp distributors to store bodies.
  * Ante-Mortem Identification Protocol: Hospital staff photographed all recovered corpses and body parts and projected them via video projector in the hospital mosque at 9:30–10:00 PM for families to identify clothing, socks, and backpacks.
  * Confirmed three hospital personnel lost their own children in the school strike.

#### 3. Activation of `EXH-WIT-007.3`: Bereaved Family Impact & Pediatric Trauma Data
* **Witness 1: Yaghoub Yazdanpanah** (Father of 6-year-old victim Fatemeh Yazdanpanah; Persian Literature Teacher, Gurzang):
  * Enrolled Fatemeh in Shajareh Tayyebeh due to her exceptional intellect and poetic gifts.
  * Described searching the rubble for three days; identified Fatemeh at 11:00 PM at Minab Hospital alongside her cousin Asra, both recovered dead in the arms of their teacher, who had died shielding them.
* **Witness 2: Dr. Mohadeseh Darvishi** (Lead Trauma Clinical Psychologist; Minab native):
  * Leading the emergency mental health response for the **283 surviving children** and their families.
  * Clinical Assessment: Severe blast-wave neurological concussion coupled with acute Post-Traumatic Stress Disorder (PTSD) and paralyzing survivor guilt.
  * Documented self-harm and trauma manifestations: surviving children using matches/lighters on their own hands/legs at the cemetery to feel the burn experienced by their classmates; pervasive nightmares of blast noises, collapsing masonry, and severed limbs; children believing U.S. forces are actively hunting them.

## 5. SUBSTANTIVE LEGAL ASSESSMENTS & REBUTTALS

### A. Mens Rea Analysis: Criminal Recklessness (*Dolus Eventualis*) under Rome Statute Art. 30
* U.S. officials (Trump, Rubio) initially framed the strike as an "unfortunate mistake" or denied U.S. responsibility entirely.
* Under international criminal law (ICTY *Galić*, ICC *Lubanga*, *Bemba*), *mens rea* encompasses not only direct intent (*dolus directus 1st degree*), but also awareness that catastrophic civilian destruction will occur in the ordinary course of events, coupled with conscious disregard of that risk (**recklessness / *dolus eventualis***).
* By deliberately dissolving civilian harm mitigation units, pushing 20,000 unvetted legacy target folders through AI pipelines (Project Maven) without human verification, and ignoring readily available Google Maps and commercial satellite data showing school children, command authorities acted with **manifest criminal recklessness**.

### B. Independent War Crime: The Secondary Strike on Shahid Absalan Medical Clinic
* The Drop Site News report establishes that at 1:00–2:00 PM on February 28, four Tomahawk missiles hit the Shahid Absalan Medical Clinic and adjacent facilities 50–80m from the school.
* Under **Geneva Convention IV, Article 18**:
  > *"Civilian hospitals organized to give care to the wounded and sick, the infirm and maternity cases, may in no circumstances be the object of attack, but shall at all times be respected and protected by the Parties to the conflict."*
* Under **Rome Statute Article 8(2)(b)(xxiv)**, intentionally directing attacks against medical units, hospitals, and personnel wearing distinctive emblems constitutes a distinct war crime. Even under a theory of dual-use or adjacency, striking an active clinic treating child victims hours after the initial strike is indefensible under the principles of distinction and precaution.

### C. International Jurisdictional Standing Validation
* In his Drop Site News interview, Iranian Justice Minister **Amin Hossein Rahimi** made two significant admissions regarding judicial strategy:
  1. Iran cannot file directly at the ICC because it is not an ICC State Party, and accepting ad hoc jurisdiction under Art. 12(3) would expose Iranian personnel to reciprocal jurisdiction.
  2. Iran is formally urging **independent international non-governmental organizations and third-party states** to submit Article 15 communications and initiate universal jurisdiction proceedings.
* **Strategic Validation:** This confirms that **People for Peace & Justice ry (PFPJ ry)**, as an independent non-governmental association incorporated in Finland, holds direct procedural standing and moral necessity to submit this dossier to the ICC Prosecutor and European national war crimes units under Universal Jurisdiction.
