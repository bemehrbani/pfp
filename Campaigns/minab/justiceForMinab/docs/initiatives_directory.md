# Global Initiatives Directory — Minab School Strike (Feb 28, 2026)
> This directory indexes active entities, NGOs, campaigns, and key groups working towards justice, accountability, documentation, or memorialization of the airstrike on the Shajareh Tayyebeh Elementary School in Minab, Iran.

---

## 1. Stars of Minab (Setareh-haye Minab)
*   **Category:** International Youth & Art Coalition
*   **Primary Channels:** 
    *   Website: [starsofminab.com](https://starsofminab.com)
    *   YouTube: [@StarsOfMinab](https://youtube.com/@StarsOfMinab)
*   **Objectives:** 
    *   Preventing the erasure of the victims' stories.
    *   Using art and narrative to highlight the "permanent absence" of the children's futures.
*   **Recent Activities:** 
    *   Organized the international exhibition *"Children of Minab Still Draw the Sun"*, featuring recovered artwork and creative tributes.
    *   Publishing high-quality visual documentation and testimonial video content.
*   **Synergy Potential:** 
    *   **Memorial Linking:** Cross-link PFP's digital memorial grid (`memorial.html`) with the Stars of Minab platform.
    *   **Creative Content:** Recruit artists from their network to contribute to the PFP memorial cards and educational graphics.
    *   **Audience Mobilization:** Leverage their YouTube subscriber base to promote PFP's Telegram Bot action campaigns.
