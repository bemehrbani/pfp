## I. Factual Determination & Operational Narrative of Case 05

### 1. Context of Strategic Crisis & Anchoring Vulnerability
On August 26, 2021, an Islamic State Khorasan Province (ISIS-K) suicide bomber detonated an explosive vest at Abbey Gate outside Hamid Karzai International Airport (HKIA) in Kabul, killing 13 U.S. service members and over 160 Afghan civilians. Operating in an environment of acute operational stress, intelligence units of U.S. Central Command (CENTCOM) and Joint Special Operations Command (JSOC) became hyper-sensitized to the threat of a secondary suicide strike, specifically anticipating a vehicle-borne improvised explosive device (VBIED) utilizing a white sedan.

### 2. The Tracking of Zamarai Ahmadi: The Mechanics of Confirmation Bias
On the morning of August 29, 2021, U.S. intelligence identified a white Toyota Corolla driven by **Zamarai Ahmadi**, a 43-year-old electrical engineer and logistical coordinator employed for 15 years by *Nutrition and Education International* (NEI), a Pasadena-based humanitarian non-profit organization. 
* At **09:00 local time**, Ahmadi left his family residence in Khwaja Burha to pick up colleagues.
* Over an eight-hour period, an MQ-9 Reaper drone tracked Ahmadi's vehicle across Kabul. Every ordinary humanitarian routine was systematically re-interpreted by targeting analysts through a preconceived hostile lens:
  * Stopping at an NGO director's residence was cataloged as visiting an "ISIS-K safe house";
  * Picking up three fellow NGO employees was interpreted as "staging co-conspirators";
  * Transporting cartons of soy distribution materials and laptop bags was tagged as "moving explosives components";
  * Loading large, heavy plastic water containers at the NEI office—necessitated because the municipal water infrastructure in his neighborhood had collapsed—was classified with "high confidence" as the loading of liquid explosives into the trunk.

### 3. The Courtyard Enclosure & The Missed Child Safeguard
At approximately **16:50 local time**, Ahmadi turned into the narrow, high-walled concrete courtyard of his extended family home. As he shifted the car into park, his sons, nephews, and nieces ran out to greet his vehicle.
* **The 2-Minute Pre-Launch Telemetry:** Full-motion video (FMV) telemetry declassified under Freedom of Information Act (FOIA) litigation proves that an MQ-9 Reaper sensor operator visually recorded the physical movement of a child entering the courtyard area approximately **two minutes prior to weapon release**.
* **Failure to Abort:** Gripped by cognitive tunnel vision and fixated upon the vehicle's engine block, the strike coordination cell failed to pause the engagement, failed to elevate the civilian sighting to the fire-control commander, and failed to issue an abort command.
* **Ordnance Release:** One AGM-114 Hellfire missile was launched. It detonated upon the front hood and engine compartment of the vehicle inside the confined courtyard.

### 4. Human Toll & Casualty Determination
The shaped-charge blast and intense thermal wave within the enclosed courtyard instantly killed **10 civilians**, including **7 children aged 2 to 12 years old**:

### Detailed Deep-Dive Analysis of the Ten Reports:

#### 1. U.S. CENTCOM Press Briefing by Gen. Kenneth F. McKenzie Jr. (Sept 17, 2021)
* **Institutional Authority:** Combatant Commander, U.S. Central Command.
* **Essential Admissions:** Gen. McKenzie formally retracted initial military assertions that the strike successfully eliminated an imminent ISIS-K bomb truck:
  * *"I am here today to set the record straight and provide the results of an investigation... Having thoroughly reviewed the findings of the investigation and the supporting analysis by interagency partners, I am now convinced that as many as 10 civilians, including up to seven children, were tragically killed in that strike."*
  * *"Moreover, we now assess that it is unlikely that the vehicle and those who died were associated with ISIS-K, or were a direct threat to U.S. forces."*
* **Forensic Reversal on Secondary Blast:** McKenzie conceded that the powerful secondary explosion initially cited as evidence of heavy explosives was likely caused by a localized rupture of Ahmadi's vehicle gas tank and a adjacent household propane tank, not a terrorist payload.

#### 2. Air Force Inspector General Investigation (Lt. Gen. Sami D. Said, Nov 3, 2021)
* **Statutory Role:** Department of the Air Force Inspector General.
* **Core Findings on Cognitive Architecture:**
  * **Confirmation Bias:** Gen. Said explicitly defined confirmation bias as the primary operational failure: analysts actively searched for evidence to confirm their hypothesis that the vehicle was hostile, while ignoring contradictory evidence indicating ordinary civilian activity.
  * **The Visual Anomaly:** Said disclosed that a post-incident frame-by-frame review of the Reaper's camera feed revealed a child present near the vehicle approximately two minutes prior to launch. However, operators testified they did not perceive the child due to screen display constraints and severe cognitive load.
  * **"Honest Mistake" Finding:** Despite acknowledging execution breakdowns, Said concluded that there was no criminal negligence, no dereliction of duty, and no violation of the law of armed conflict.

#### 3. The New York Times Visual Forensic Investigation (Sept 10, 2021)
* **Methodology:** Frame-by-frame analysis of commercial security cameras, architectural 3D photogrammetric modeling of the courtyard, interviews with NEI staff, and structural blast calculations.
* **Key Forensic Breakthroughs:**
  * Disproved CENTCOM's claim that Ahmadi visited an ISIS safe house; established the address was the office of NEI founder Dr. Steven Kwon.
  * Proved through office water spigot footage that Ahmadi filled 5-gallon plastic jugs with drinking water because local neighborhood taps were non-functional.
  * Conducted crater and wall spallation analysis proving the blast wave lacked the overpressure signature of hundreds of pounds of ammonium nitrate, definitively discrediting the VBIED claim.

#### 4. CENTCOM Declassified Redacted AR 15-6 Investigation Records (Jan 2022)
* **Primary Evidence:** 66 declassified pages of mIRC targeting chat logs, target development packets, and witness interviews.
* **Critical Operational Takeaways:**
  * Shows the total absence of real-time red-teaming or contrarian vetting within the Over-the-Horizon strike cell.
  * Exposes that the target nomination was validated within a compressed 4-hour window where analysts were under direct pressure to produce strikeable targets before the final evacuation flight departed.

#### 5. Just Security Legal Critique (Haque, Goodman & Finucane, Sept/Nov 2021)
* **IHL Doctrinal Assessment:**
  * Demolishes the Air Force IG's assertion that an "honest mistake" precludes a violation of the law of war.
  * Establishes that under **AP I Article 57(2)(a)(i)**, an attacking force has an affirmative legal duty to do "everything feasible" to verify that the target is a military objective. Feasibility is measured objectively against the capabilities of a reasonable commander equipped with advanced remote surveillance assets.
  * Applies **AP I Article 52(3)**: In cases of doubt, a vehicle or structure customarily dedicated to civilian use must be presumed civilian. In Kabul, targeting personnel unlawfully inverted this presumption, placing the burden on the civilian to prove they were not an insurgent.

#### 6. Lieber Institute West Point: "Cognitive Bias and LOAC" (Meier & Nasu, Nov 2021)
* **Military Jurisprudence:**
  * Examines the interaction between human cognitive limitations and legal duties of precaution.
  * Argues that confirmation bias is not an unforeseeable act of God; it is a well-documented human factor in high-stress combat operations. Consequently, a military command that fails to institute procedural redundancies (such as devil's advocates or red teams) fails to satisfy the customary LOAC standard of feasible precautions.

#### 7. Lawfare: "Target Selection and Presumption of Civilian Status" (Margulies & Jenks, 2021)
* **Legal Standards:**
  * Rebuts the defense that "high threat environments" lower the standard of positive identification (PID).
  * Analyzes the Rome Statute Article 30 mental element: when targeting personnel become aware that their target hypothesis rests upon ambiguous assumptions (such as plastic jugs), persisting with kinetic release constitutes conditional intent (*dolus eventualis*).

#### 8. Human Rights Watch Report: "US: No Accountability" (Dec 14, 2021)
* **Institutional Critique:**
  * Documents the structural impunity inherent in military self-investigations where no officer is held administratively or criminally accountable.
  * Criticizes the Department of Defense for offering voluntary *ex gratia* payments while failing to recognize a binding legal obligation of state restitution under international law.

#### 9. Amnesty International Briefing: "Flawed Investigation Ensures Impunity" (Nov 2021)
* **International Criminal Law:**
  * Petitions the International Criminal Court (ICC) to incorporate drone strikes with child casualties into its Afghan investigation under Article 54 of the Rome Statute.
  * Highlights that child casualties in residential sanctuaries require independent judicial adjudication, not unilateral military administrative closure.

#### 10. UNAMA / UN OHCHR Report A/HRC/49/24 (March 2022)
* **Intergovernmental Authority:**
  * Establishes state responsibility for extraterritorial aerial strikes resulting in civilian deaths.
  * Affirms that customary IHL requires transparent public disclosure of targeting dossiers and independent judicial mechanisms to assess state liability.

## III. Prior Jurisprudence & Canonical Cases Cited by the Kabul Inquiries

The legal inquiries and doctrinal reviews of Case 05 heavily cite foundational international decisions that directly strengthen our Minab Report:

1. **ICTY Appeals Chamber, *Prosecutor v. Stanislav Galić* (Case No. IT-98-29-A, Judgment of 30 November 2006, ¶¶ 140–144):**
   * *Holding on Indiscriminate Attacks and Recklessness:* The Appeals Chamber affirmed that an attack directed against civilians can be inferred from the weapon type, blast radius, and total disregard for civilian presence. The *mens rea* for war crimes encompasses not only direct malicious intent, but also **culpable recklessness and conditional intent (*dolus eventualis*)**, where the perpetrator was aware of the likelihood of civilian casualties and proceeded nonetheless.
2. **ICTY Trial Chamber, *Prosecutor v. Zoran Kupreškić et al.* (Case No. IT-95-16-T, Judgment of 14 January 2000, ¶ 524):**
   * *Holding on the Primacy of Civilian Immunity:* The Chamber held that elementary considerations of humanity prohibit attacks against civilian population centers, emphasizing that military necessity can never override the peremptory obligation of distinction.
3. **ICTY OTP Final Report on NATO Bombing Campaign Against FRY (June 2000, ¶¶ 80–85):**
   * *The Belgrade Chinese Embassy Benchmark:* Formulates the definitive three-stage test for upstream target nomination failure, holding that targeting databases contaminated by flawed peacetime methodology cannot be excused by the "fog of war."
4. **ECtHR Grand Chamber, *Hanan v. Germany* (App. No. 4871/16, Judgment of 16 February 2021, §§ 132–145):**
   * *Holding on Procedural Investigative Obligation:* Establishes that where a State executes an extraterritorial aerial strike that inflicts mass civilian casualties, an autonomous procedural obligation under Article 2 of the ECHR is triggered, requiring an independent, effective, and prompt criminal investigation capable of determining command liability.
5. **ECtHR, *Isayeva, Yusupova and Bazayeva v. Russia* (App. No. 57947/00, Judgment of 24 February 2005, §§ 171, 187–188, 195–199):**
   * *Holding on Daytime Aerial Attacks & Failure of Verification:* Military authorities cannot plead ignorance of a prominent civilian presence during daylight hours; launching heavy ordnance into populated areas without positive verification violates the non-derogable right to life.
