## 1. Executive Factual Summary
* **Date & Micro-Time:** Thursday, 16 March 2017, at approximately 18:55 local time (dusk interval between *Maghrib* and *Isha* prayers).
* **Geographic Locus:** Omar Ibn al-Khattab Mosque Complex, Al-Jinah village, Western Aleppo countryside, Syrian Arab Republic (`36.1425° N, 36.8483° E`).
* **Weaponry Deployed:** Strike package of ten (10) GBU-39 Small Diameter Bombs (SDB) and two (2) 500-lb JDAMs delivered by F-15E strike fighters; followed by a secondary salvo of two (2) AGM-114 Hellfire blast-fragmentation missiles fired by an MQ-9 Reaper unmanned combat aerial vehicle.
* **Intended Target vs. Actual Object:** USCENTCOM targeted what intelligence classified as an "Al-Qaeda in Syria meeting location" adjacent to a mosque. The actual structure struck was the active, multi-story service hall and educational annexe of the Omar Ibn al-Khattab Mosque, housing approximately 250 to 300 civilian worshippers and children gathered for weekly religious instruction (*Tablighi Jamaat*) and an evening communal meal.
* **Casualties:** Between 38 and 49 certified civilian fatalities (including at least 5 children); dozens permanently maimed and wounded; zero verified al-Qaeda leadership casualties.

1. **Failure of Temporal Verification across Multi-Day Custody:** CENTCOM targeting teams had information concerning the site three days prior to the attack, but failed to initiate target development until the day of execution. Planners failed to conduct pre-strike pattern-of-life surveillance during dusk hours, remaining oblivious to the regular Thursday evening gathering.
2. **Omission from the No-Strike Entity (NSE) Register:** The service building had not been catalogued onto the military's localized No-Strike List (NSL). The strike cell treated the negative query result as positive authorization, failing to cross-reference commercial imagery or open cadastral data.
3. **Institutional Shift-Handover Disorganization:** As officially admitted in the CENTCOM AR 15-6 briefing, communication failures during shift changeovers led to a total loss of situational awareness regarding the religious character of the building, which military briefers formally characterized as an unforced, *"preventable error."*
4. **Follow-On Strikes on Fleeing Survivors:** Following the initial structural collapse, an MQ-9 Reaper drone targeted individuals fleeing the building onto surrounding roadways with two AGM-114 Hellfire missiles armed with external fragmentation sleeves, killing fleeing non-combatants and rescuers.

## 4. Key Precedential Holdings & Principles of International Humanitarian Law

### A. The Principle of Continuous Temporal Target Verification (AP I Art. 57; CIHL Rule 15)
Target verification is an affirmative, continuous legal duty. Where an armed force maintains intelligence custody over a target for an extended duration, the obligation to confirm the civilian or military character of the facility intensifies up to the moment of attack. Historical or static intelligence cannot justify kinetic execution without real-time pattern-of-life confirmation.

### B. The Non-Severability of Integrated Protected Compounds (AP I Art. 52(3))
An attacking military cannot arbitrarily sever an auxiliary building, service wing, or administrative annexe from a protected educational, religious, or medical complex to claim that an adjacent structure was struck in good faith. Under Article 52(3) of Additional Protocol I, in case of doubt, the absolute presumption of civilian status controls.

### C. Proximity Heightens—Rather than Relieves—Precautionary Duties
Proximity to an adversary military position or contested perimeter does not excuse an attacker's failure to detect active civilian use. On the contrary, physical proximity to a protected object triggers a heightened standard of scrutiny *strictissimi juris*, rendering reliance on negative database entries (omission from a no-strike list) gross operational negligence.

### D. Systemic Institutional Negligence Imputes Direct State Responsibility
Administrative disorganization, shift-handover miscommunications, and the dismantling of dedicated civilian harm review units within an automated strike cell are not excusable "fog of war" occurrences. Under **Article 4 of the ILC Articles on State Responsibility (ARSIWA)**, institutional negligence within military command organs engages the unconditional international responsibility of the State.

### E. Absolute Prohibition of Secondary Kinetic Strikes on Evacuating Civilians
Directing follow-on precision munitions into escape routes, emergency stairwells, or assembly courtyards where non-combatants are fleeing constitutes an unlawful indiscriminate attack under **Article 51(4) of Additional Protocol I**, demonstrating reckless disregard for human life (*dolus eventualis*).
