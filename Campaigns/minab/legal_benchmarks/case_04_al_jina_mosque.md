# Case Dossier 04: Al-Jina Mosque Strike (UN HRC / Syria)
> **Database ID:** PFPJ-LB-004  
> **Applicable Aspects:** A (Precision Weaponry), B (Targeting Error), C (Protected Infrastructure), D (Proportionality & Proximity)

---

## 1. Incident Overview
*   **Date:** March 16, 2017
*   **Location:** Al-Jina, Aleppo Governorate, Syria
*   **Weapon Used:** AGM-114 Hellfire missiles and JDAM precision bombs.
*   **Casualties:** 38+ civilians killed, including children.

---

## 2. Factual Breakdown & Targeting Error
US aircraft targeted a building in the village of Al-Jina, which military intelligence had identified as a "non-mosque Al-Qaeda meeting hall." The building was actually a newly constructed extension of a village mosque, filled with civilian worshippers gathered for evening lectures. 

The US military claimed they did not have updated coordinates showing that the building was part of the mosque complex, and claimed a targeting error based on outdated satellite intelligence and local patterns of life.

## 3. Legal Proceedings & Public Reports
*   **Investigating Body:** UN Human Rights Council Independent International Commission of Inquiry on the Syrian Arab Republic.
*   **Public Report:** *Report of the Independent International Commission of Inquiry on the Syrian Arab Republic (A/HRC/36/55)*.
*   **Public Report URL:** [UN HRC Syria Inquiry Documentation Portal](https://www.ohchr.org/en/hr-bodies/hrc/co-i-syria/documentation)

---

## 4. Key Legal Precedents & Rationale
*   **Failure of Precaution in Target Verification:** The UN Commission of Inquiry concluded that the US military **failed to take all feasible precautions** to verify that the target was a military objective, in violation of customary International Humanitarian Law (IHL).
*   **Duty to Check Coordinate Shifts Over Time:** The UN noted that the US military failed to account for recent construction and usage changes. Planners have a continuous duty to verify the active civilian status of infrastructure prior to authorization.

---

## 5. Direct Application to the Minab Case
*   **Target Verification Standard:** The US defense that they did not know the school was active next to the IRGC base is identical to the US defense in Al-Jina. We can use the UN's legal reasoning to argue that **failing to verify coordinate changes over time** (the active school) is a direct breach of the customary IHL duty of precaution, rendering the strike unlawful and establishing grounds for command negligence.
