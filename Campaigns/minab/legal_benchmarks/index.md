## 2. Expanded Cases & Reports Registry

The following 12 reports form our extended comparative registry, identifying further targeting error cases globally:

### Case 07: Dasht-e-Archi Madrassa Strike (Afghanistan, 2018)
*   **Source:** *UNAMA Human Rights Special Report — Airstrikes in Dasht-e-Archi (May 2018)*
*   **Aspects:** B, C, D
*   **Context:** Afghan forces struck a religious school (madrassa) during a graduation ceremony. Over 100 casualties (mostly children). 
*   **Precedent:** Establishes the UN's strict standard of proof regarding **proportionality** when children are present, rejecting claims of collateral necessity.
*   **URL:** [UNAMA Dasht-e-Archi Special Report](https://unmissions.org/unama-special-report-airstrikes-dasht-e-archi-district-kunduz-province)

### Case 08: Al-Amiriyah Shelter Bombing (Iraq, 1991)
*   **Source:** *Human Rights Watch Middle East Report (1991)*
*   **Aspects:** A, B, C, F
*   **Context:** US F-117 stealth aircraft dropped two laser-guided smart bombs on a civilian bomb shelter, killing 408 civilians (mostly women and children). The US claimed the shelter was identified as an active military command bunker.
*   **Precedent:** Standard for demonstrating that **misinterpreting civilian shelters/schools** as military bunkers is an indictment of targeting vetting.
*   **URL:** [Human Rights Watch Al-Amiriyah Strike Documentation](https://www.hrw.org/reports/1991/iraq/)

### Case 09: Qana Refugee Compound Massacre (Lebanon, 1996)
*   **Source:** *UN Van Kappen Investigation Report (S/1996/337)*
*   **Aspects:** B, C, D
*   **Context:** Israeli artillery struck a UN compound sheltering 800 displaced civilians, killing 106. Israel claimed targeting coordinates were misaligned during counter-fire.
*   **Precedent:** Establishes that **shelling international compounds/schools** cannot be dismissed as an error if simple visual and database coordination was available.
*   **URL:** [UN Van Kappen Qana Report S/1996/337](https://undocs.org/S/1996/337)

### Case 10: Second Qana residential strike (Lebanon, 2006)
*   **Source:** *Human Rights Watch Report — Fatal Strikes (August 2006)*
*   **Aspects:** A, B, C, D
*   **Context:** An Israeli airstrike hit a three-story residential building, killing 28 civilians (including 16 children). The IDF claimed the building was a rocket launching site.
*   **Precedent:** Landmark analysis on the **duty to verify target occupancy** in real-time.
*   **URL:** [HRW Fatal Strikes Qana 2006](https://www.hrw.org/report/2006/08/30/fatal-strikes/israels-indiscriminate-attacks-lebanon-2006)

### Case 11: NATO RTS TV Station Strike (Belgrade, 1999)
*   **Source:** *ECtHR Decision — Bankovic and Others v. Belgium (2001)*
*   **Aspects:** A, C, D, E, F
*   **Context:** NATO aircraft struck the Belgrade headquarters of the state broadcaster, killing 16 civilians. NATO claimed the media building was a military propaganda target.
*   **Precedent:** Landmark case exploring **extraterritorial jurisdiction** and defining "civilian objects" in international law.
*   **URL:** [ECtHR Bankovic v. Belgium Decision](https://hudoc.echr.coe.int/eng?i=001-22099)

### Case 12: NATO Korisa Village Strike (Kosovo, 1999)
*   **Source:** *ICTY OTP NATO Bombing Campaign Report (Paragraphs 86–89)*
*   **Aspects:** B, D, F
*   **Context:** NATO bombed a facility believing it was an active military camp, killing 87 Kosovo Albanian refugees who were being used as human shields or sheltering there.
*   **Precedent:** Evaluates the **Principle of Proportionality** when military forces fail to confirm civilian presence next to military coordinates.
*   **URL:** [ICTY Korisa Village Paragraphs](https://www.icty.org/en/press/final-report-prosecutor-committee-established-review-nato-bombing-campaign-against-federal#IV)

### Case 13: Hass School Complex Strike (Syria, 2016)
*   **Source:** *Joint HRW & Bellingcat OSINT Investigation (October 2016)*
*   **Aspects:** B, C, D
*   **Context:** High-altitude airstrikes hit multiple school yards in Hass, killing 22 children. The Russian/Syrian commands denied responsibility.
*   **Precedent:** Benchmark for using **flight-tracking telemetry and shadow chronolocation** to attribute blame.
*   **URL:** [Bellingcat Hass School Strike Verification](https://www.bellingcat.com/news/mena/2016/10/27/video-verification-hass-school-bombing/)

### Case 14: Abs Hospital Strike (Yemen, 2016)
*   **Source:** *Joint Incident Assessment Team (JIAT) Coalition Report (2016)*
*   **Aspects:** A, B, C
*   **Context:** A Saudi Coalition jet struck a hospital supported by MSF, killing 19. The JIAT claimed the pilot targeted a vehicle fleeing near the hospital (spillover error).
*   **Precedent:** Examines **strict pilot verification rules** when targeting moving objects adjacent to medical/educational facilities.
*   **URL:** [JIAT Coalition Assessment Reports](https://www.jiat-yemen.org/)

### Case 15: Dahyan School Bus Strike (Yemen, 2018)
*   **Source:** *UN Group of Eminent Experts on Yemen Report (September 2019)*
*   **Aspects:** A, B, C, D
*   **Context:** A Saudi-led coalition jet dropped a US-manufactured GBU-12 laser-guided bomb on a school bus in a market, killing 40 children. The coalition claimed it was targeting Houthi leaders.
*   **Precedent:** Illustrates the **illegal use of precision weapons** against high-density child civilian targets.
*   **URL:** [UN Group of Experts Yemen Reports](https://www.ohchr.org/en/hr-bodies/hrc/yemen/group-of-eminent-experts)

### Case 16: Al-Bara School Strike (Syria, 2013)
*   **Source:** *Human Rights Watch Report — Death from the Sky (2013)*
*   **Aspects:** B, C, D
*   **Context:** A Syrian military jet dropped bombs directly onto a schoolyard during class hours, killing 8 children.
*   **Precedent:** Precedent on **deliberate or reckless target selection** of educational sites.
*   **URL:** [HRW Death from the Sky Syria](https://www.hrw.org/report/2013/04/10/death-sky/airstrikes-civilian-areas-syria)

### Case 17: Chernihiv School Bombing (Ukraine, 2022)
*   **Source:** *Amnesty International War Crimes Investigation (March 2022)*
*   **Aspects:** B, C, F
*   **Context:** Russian airstrikes struck two school buildings and residential areas in Chernihiv on Day 7 of the invasion, killing 47 civilians.
*   **Precedent:** Focuses on the **use of unguided bombs in populated areas** during the opening week of an invasion.
*   **URL:** [Amnesty International Chernihiv Investigation](https://www.amnesty.org/en/latest/news/2022/03/ukraine-russian-airstrike-kills-civilians-in-chernihiv-new-investigation/)

### Case 18: Mastaba Market Strike (Yemen, 2016)
*   **Source:** *Human Rights Watch Report — Airstrikes on Mastaba Market (2016)*
*   **Aspects:** A, B, D
*   **Context:** Two US-made GBU-31 JDAM bombs struck a crowded market next to a Houthi military checkpoint, killing 97 civilians (including 25 children).
*   **Precedent:** Proves that **striking next to minor military check-points** violates proportionality when high civilian density is predictable.
*   **URL:** [HRW Mastaba Market Investigation](https://www.hrw.org/news/2016/04/07/yemen-us-bombs-used-deadly-market-strikes)
