## 1. Incident Overview & Operational Demarcation
* **Date & Micro-Time:** August 29, 2021, at approximately 16:50 local Kabul time (UTC+4:30).
* **Geographic Locus:** Residential courtyard, Khwaja Burha neighborhood, District 15, Kabul, Afghanistan (Coordinates: 34°33'44.8"N, 69°10'33.7"E).
* **Launch Platform & Ordnance:** General Atomics MQ-9 Reaper remotely piloted aircraft (RPA), deploying one AGM-114 Hellfire air-to-ground precision-guided missile equipped with a shaped-charge high-explosive antitank (HEAT) / blast warhead.
* **Command & Operational Architecture:** United States Central Command (US CENTCOM) Over-the-Horizon Strike Cell, in coordination with Joint Special Operations Task Force (JSOTF) and Combined Forces Air Component Command (CFACC).
* **Nominated / Intended Target:** An alleged Islamic State Khorasan Province (ISIS-K) facilitator operating a white Toyota Corolla, purported to be transporting a vehicle-borne improvised explosive device (VBIED) to execute an imminent suicide bombing at the Abbey Gate / Hamid Karzai International Airport (HKIA) evacuation corridor.
* **Actual Target Ground Truth:** A civilian passenger vehicle owned and driven by **Zamarai Ahmadi** (43), a longtime logistical engineer and humanitarian aid worker for *Nutrition and Education International* (NEI), a Pasadena-based non-profit humanitarian organization distributing soy products to malnourished Afghan women and children.
* **Human Casualties:** 10 civilians killed instantly or succumbing to catastrophic burn/fragmentation trauma in the enclosed courtyard—**including 7 children aged 2 to 12 years old**; zero combatants or military objectives present.

## 3. Operational Mechanics & Cognitive Pathology of the Kabul Strike
1. **The Anchoring Trap & Confirmation Bias:** Following the horrific Abbey Gate bombing of August 26, 2021, CENTCOM intelligence was anchored on an imminent second strike utilizing a white Toyota Corolla. When Ahmadi’s vehicle was visually spotted near a suspected ISIS-K safe house, analysts formed an unbreakable cognitive hypothesis that Ahmadi was an ISIS operative.
2. **The "Water Jug" Fallacy:** Ahmadi’s humanitarian office lacked running municipal water. Security cameras recorded him filling large plastic water jugs from an office spigot to bring home to his family. Remote drone analysts, observing from thousands of miles away, interpreted the heavy containers as liquid explosives and ammonium nitrate.
3. **The Sensor Resolution Paradox:** While the MQ-9 Reaper provided crystal-clear full-motion video (FMV), analysts suffered from extreme "soda-straw" tunnel vision. High visual resolution created an unwarranted illusion of total situational understanding, masking the reality that the subjects were humanitarian workers performing mundane administrative duties.
4. **The Pre-Launch Child Detection Breakdown:** Approximately two minutes prior to missile launch, sensor telemetry captured a young child entering the vehicle's perimeter. Because the strike cell was fixated on the vehicle's engine block and committed to weapons release, this critical observation was never escalated or communicated to the fire-control commander, directly violating the duty to suspend or cancel attacks under AP I Article 57(2)(b).

## 5. Doctrinal and Judicial Applications to the Minab Master Dossier
* **Rebuttal of the "Honest Mistake" Defense:** Citing the scholarly consensus from *Just Security*, *Lieber Institute*, and *Lawfare*, the Minab Report establishes that subjective good faith does not excuse gross operational failure. Under customary IHL, a mistake of fact is excusable only if it was reasonable under the circumstances. Launching three 1,000-lb cruise missiles into an urban area without querying civilian cadaster or daytime satellite passes is gross negligence.
* **Presumption of Civilian Status (*In Dubio Pro Civitate*):** Applying AP I Article 52(3), in case of doubt whether a building dedicated to civilian purposes (such as education) is being used to make an effective contribution to military action, it shall be presumed not to be so used. In Minab, CENTCOM inverted this presumption by substituting algorithmic probability scores for positive identification.
* **The Non-Delegable Duty of Target Verification:** Human commanders cannot shield themselves behind the Maven-Claude AI pipeline. Just as the Air Force IG held that targeting personnel cannot ignore civilian presence on remote sensors, commanders cannot outsource positive target identification to high-speed algorithms.
