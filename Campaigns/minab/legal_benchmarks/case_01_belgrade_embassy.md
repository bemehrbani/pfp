# Case Dossier 01: Chinese Embassy Bombing (Belgrade, 1999)
> **Database ID:** PFPJ-LB-001  
> **Applicable Aspects:** A (Precision Weaponry), B (Targeting Error), C (Protected Infrastructure), F (Day 1 / Pre-Planned Peacetime Nominations)

---

## 1. Incident Overview
*   **Date:** May 7, 1999
*   **Location:** Belgrade, Federal Republic of Yugoslavia (FRY)
*   **Weapon Used:** JDAM GPS precision-guided bombs (launched from US B-2 bombers).
*   **Casualties:** 3 Chinese journalists killed, 20 embassy staff injured.

---

## 2. Factual Breakdown & Targeting Error
During NATO's bombing campaign in Yugoslavia, three precision bombs hit the Chinese Embassy in Belgrade. The US military and CIA claimed a severe "targeting error." 

The intended target was the Federal Directorate for Supply and Procurement (FDSP), a military supply facility. The CIA conducted the targeting process using **outdated maps** (a 1997 tourist map and outdated database records) that did not reflect that the Chinese Embassy had relocated to the target site. The target verification procedures failed to double-check the geographic shifts of the foreign diplomatic mission.

---

## 3. Legal Proceedings & Public Reports
*   **Investigating Body:** International Criminal Tribunal for the former Yugoslavia (ICTY) Office of the Prosecutor (OTP).
*   **Public Report:** *Final Report to the Prosecutor by the Committee Established to Review the NATO Bombing Campaign Against the Federal Republic of Yugoslavia* (Paragraphs 80–85).
*   **Public Report URL:** [ICTY Official NATO Bombing Report](https://www.icty.org/en/press/final-report-prosecutor-committee-established-review-nato-bombing-campaign-against-federal)

---

## 4. Key Legal Precedents & Rationale
*   **Target Verification Obligation:** The ICTY committee accepted that the attack was an accidental error, meaning there was no criminal intent (*mens rea*) to target a diplomatic compound. However, the report established that **reliance on outdated intelligence data violates military standards of target verification** and constitutes structural command failure.
*   **The Peacetime Planning Standard:** Because the target was selected during the pre-planned phase (rather than a rapid tactical strike in active combat), the standard of diligence required for target verification was significantly higher.

---

## 5. Direct Application to the Minab Case
*   **Peacetime Target Planning:** In the Minab school strike, the US claimed the school was struck due to outdated intelligence identifying it as part of the IRGC base. Because this occurred on Day 1 of the US-Iran war, the target list was compiled in peacetime. Under the Belgrade precedent, this creates a strong case for **systemic negligence in target verification**, since the planning command had months during peacetime to verify that the coordinate was an active elementary school rather than a military base.
