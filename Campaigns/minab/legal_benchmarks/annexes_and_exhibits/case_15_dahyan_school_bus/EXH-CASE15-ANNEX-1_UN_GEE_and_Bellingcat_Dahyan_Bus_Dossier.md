## I. Summary of the Incident & Verified Casualty Roster

1. **Date & Timestamp:** Thursday, 9 August 2018, between 08:30 and 08:45 AST.
2. **Location:** Commercial Marketplace, Dahyan, Majz District, Sa'ada Governorate, Yemen (`17.0694° N, 43.6192° E`).
3. **Vehicle Profile:** Blue civilian transport bus, single-deck, 30 passenger capacity, carrying 54 schoolboys aged 6 to 13 returning from a summer Quranic camp excursion.
4. **Verified Casualties:**
   - **Fatalities:** 51 individuals certified deceased, comprising 40 schoolchildren and 11 adult civilians (teachers, drivers, and market bystanders).
   - **Injuries:** 79 individuals documented with traumatic injuries, comprising 56 children and 23 adults. Traumatic injuries included traumatic bilateral limb amputations, open cranial fractures, shrapnel penetrations of the thorax and abdomen, and third-degree blast burns.
5. **Hospital Registry Data:**
   - **Al-Talh Hospital (ICRC-supported):** Received 29 dead children and 48 wounded persons (including 30 children) within the first 120 minutes post-strike.
   - **Al-Jumhori Hospital (Sa'ada City):** Received 16 fatalities and 31 wounded persons.

## III. UN Group of Eminent Experts (UN GEE) Formal Findings (UN Doc. A/HRC/42/17 & A/HRC/42/CRP.1)

The Group of Eminent International and Regional Experts on Yemen examined the Dahyan strike in detail (A/HRC/42/17, paras 25, 30, 82; and detailed findings in A/HRC/42/CRP.1, paras 449–450, 896–905):

1. **Failure of Distinction (A/HRC/42/17, para 25):** The Group determined that the bus was an exclusively civilian conveyance carrying young schoolchildren returning from an educational trip. The Group found no evidence that the bus was being used for military purposes or that combatants were present on board.
2. **Failure of Precautions (A/HRC/42/CRP.1, paras 449–450):** The Group emphasized that even if the Coalition believed the bus had carried combatants at some earlier point, the attacking force was under an affirmative obligation under customary IHL and AP I Article 57 to verify the target in real time. Because the strike occurred in broad daylight in an open market packed with civilians, surveillance assets had ample opportunity to observe the high concentration of children.
3. **Breach of the Abort Duty (A/HRC/42/CRP.1, paras 896–898):** The Group highlighted that the obligation to cancel or suspend an attack under AP I Article 57(2)(b) is mandatory whenever it becomes apparent that the objective is civilian or that civilian harm would be disproportionate. The failure to abort the weapon release upon the bus entering Dahyan market constitutes an actionable breach of IHL.
4. **War Crime Conclusion (A/HRC/42/17, para 82):** The Group concluded that there are reasonable grounds to believe that the airstrike on the Dahyan market school bus was an indiscriminate and disproportionate attack, giving rise to individual criminal responsibility for **war crimes**.

## IV. Joint Incident Assessment Team (JIAT) Official Statement (September 1, 2018)

On 1 September 2018, the Coalition's Joint Incident Assessment Team held an official televised press conference in Riyadh, led by Legal Advisor Mansour Ahmed Al-Mansour. The findings constitute a formal declaration against interest by the belligerent party:

1. **Finding of Fault:** JIAT officially acknowledged that the airstrike on the bus was **"unjustified"** and resulted from a **"failure to comply with the Coalition's Rules of Engagement (ROE)"**.
2. **The "Operational Delay" Admission:** JIAT stated that intelligence had nominated the bus as a target earlier in the day based on suspected Houthi leaders, but an operational delay occurred in mission execution. By the time the strike was cleared and the bomb released, the bus had reached a crowded market area where it no longer posed an imminent threat to Coalition forces.
3. **Failure of Precautionary Abort:** JIAT acknowledged that the command and aircrew failed to abort the attack once the vehicle was in the vicinity of civilian concentrations, causing massive collateral damage.
4. **Formal Recommendations of JIAT:**
   - The Coalition must officially apologize for the strike;
   - Disciplinary action and judicial prosecution must be initiated against those responsible for the error;
   - Comprehensive financial compensation and redress must be paid to the bereaved families and wounded victims.
