# Part 1: Structural Extraction from Benchmark Reports

To ensure our report meets the strict criteria of international courts and European prosecutors, we have analyzed and captured the structures of the following public reports:

### 1. The Prosecutorial Evaluation Structure (ICTY OTP NATO Campaign Report)
*   **Structural Layout:**
    *   **Introduction:** Explains the mandate of the investigating committee, the scope of review, and standard of evaluation.
    *   **General Issues:** Evaluates weapon categories (e.g., cluster munitions) and general target selection protocols.
    *   **Specific Incidents Section:** Follows a strict tripartite sequence:
        1.  *Factual Background:* Detail of the strike, dates, targets, casualties.
        2.  *Factual and Legal Issues:* Evaluates whether the target was a legitimate military objective, whether proportionality was respected, and if all precautions were taken.
        3.  *Prosecutorial Conclusion:* Recommendation on whether to initiate criminal proceedings.
*   **Key Takeaway for Minab:**
    *   Our "Incident Narrative" must separate the *factual sequence* from the *legal analysis* of precaution. This ensures that the facts are established objectively before we apply the IHL standards of negligence.

### 2. The Judicial Trial Framework (ECtHR Grand Chamber: Hanan v. Germany)
*   **Structural Layout:**
    *   **The Facts:**
        1.  *The circumstances of the case:* Full narrative of the airstrike.
        2.  *Domestic investigations:* A step-by-step review of how the German Federal Prosecutor handled the case.
        3.  *Relevant International Law:* Citations of Geneva Conventions, Customary IHL, and ECHR Articles.
    *   **The Law:**
        1.  *Jurisdictional link:* Establishes whether the state had jurisdiction over the target/actors (ECHR Article 1).
        2.  *Procedural obligation to investigate:* Evaluates whether the domestic investigation was independent, prompt, thorough, and transparent (ECHR Article 2).
*   **Key Takeaway for Minab:**
    *   Because our filing is going to a European public prosecutor (Finland/Germany), we must include a dedicated **Jurisdiction and Procedural Obligation** section. We must prove that under ECHR jurisprudence, Finland has a binding duty to investigate war crimes coordinated or supported by resources/entities under its jurisdiction.

### 3. The International Fact-Finding Commission Structure (UN HRC Al-Jina Mosque Report)
*   **Structural Layout:**
    *   **Context & Mandate:** Background of the conflict and Commission authority.
    *   **Methodology & Verification:** Explains the use of satellite imagery, witness interviews, and open-source geolocation.
    *   **Incident Documentation:** Maps geo-coordinates, construction timelines, and civilian usage cycles of the hit target.
    *   **Substantive Legal Findings:** Direct application of:
        1.  *Principle of Distinction* (Civilian vs. Military objects).
        2.  *Principle of Proportionality* (Collateral damage vs. Military advantage).
        3.  *Principle of Precaution* (Feasible measures to verify target).
*   **Key Takeaway for Minab:**
    *   The UN structure shows that **spatial analysis** is critical. For Minab, we must visually and legally document the exact coordinate partition between the school and the adjacent IRGC base to prove a violation of the Principle of Precaution.

### 4. The Military Inquiry Structure (US CENTCOM Kabul Drone Strike Report)
*   **Structural Layout:**
    *   **Executive Summary:** High-level narrative of the strike and the "tragic mistake" admission.
    *   **Mission Profile & Command Chain:** Identifies the units, drone operators, and commanding officers who approved the strike.
    *   **Chronological Factual Log:** The timestamped sequence of the target tracking loop.
    *   **Intelligence Vetting & Breakdown:** Analyzes the confirmation bias, misidentified objects, and failure of real-time precaution checks.
*   **Key Takeaway for Minab:**
    *   To indict the US command chain, we must reconstruct the **Target Vetting Loop**. We need to prove that the intelligence analysts suffered from confirmation bias, assuming any movement near the IRGC base was hostile, and failed to check the active school registry.

# Part 2: Proposed Structure for the Minab Incident Report

We propose the following comprehensive structure for the final **Minab Factual Determination & Court-Ready Report**:

## Title: REPORT ON THE FACTUAL DETERMINATION AND COMMAND NEGLIGENCE CONCERNING THE FEBRUARY 28, 2026 AIRSTRIKE ON THE SHAJAREH TAYYEBEH SCHOOL IN MINAB, IRAN

---

### SECTION 1: INTRODUCTION & EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
*   **1.1 Mandate of the Report:** Formally state PFPJ ry's mission to document violations of international law against children.
*   **1.2 Brief Factual Summary:** The date (Feb 28, 2026), location (Minab, Iran), weapon (Tomahawk cruise missile), and casualties (168 children killed).
*   **1.3 Primary Conclusion:** Characterization of the strike as a war crime resulting from command negligence in target planning, rejecting the "honest mistake" defense.

### SECTION 2: INVESTIGATIVE METHODOLOGY & EVIDENCE VERIFICATION
*   **2.1 Sources of Information:** Detail our use of open-source intelligence (OSINT), satellite imagery, media reports, and victim registry verification.
*   **2.2 Standard of Proof:** Define the **"Reasonable grounds to believe"** standard used to evaluate the evidence.
*   **2.3 Digital Evidence Authentication:** Document the process used to split, verify, and verify the identities and photos of the 100 children in the memorial database.
    *   *Reference Benchmarks:* Cite **Hass School Strike (Case 13)** for OSINT chronolocation standards.

### SECTION 3: CHRONOLOGY & GEOGRAPHIC ANALYSIS OF THE TARGET
*   **3.1 Spatial Analysis & Partition:** Detail the exact coordinates of the Shajareh Tayyebeh Girls' Elementary School and the adjacent IRGC naval base. Document the physical fence boundary.
    *   *Required Evidence:* High-resolution satellite images showing the school's layout.
*   **3.2 Peacetime Coordinate History:** Prove that the school was established, registered, and active for years prior to 2026, meaning its location was publicly discoverable.
*   **3.3 The Strike Timeline:** Reconstruct the timestamped sequence of the strike on February 28, 2026 (Day 1 of the hostilities).

### SECTION 4: APPLICABLE INTERNATIONAL LAW
*   **4.1 Treaties & Conventions:** Cite Geneva Convention IV (Protection of Civilians), Additional Protocol I (Articles 48, 51, 52, 57), and Rome Statute (Article 8).
*   **4.2 Special Protection of Educational Facilities:** Detail the legal framework of the *Safe Schools Declaration* and the heightened protection afforded to children in armed conflict.

### SECTION 5: LEGAL LIABILITY & NEGLECT OF PRECAUTION
*   **5.1 Violation of the Principle of Precaution (Target Verification):** Argue that the US military failed to take all feasible measures to verify the target.
    *   *Reference Benchmarks:* Cite **Al-Jina Mosque (Case 04)** to show that failing to verify target coordinate changes over time constitutes a breach of the duty of precaution.
*   **5.2 Rejection of the "Fog of War" / "Honest Mistake" Defense:** Explain that because this strike occurred on Day 1, the target was drawn from a pre-planned peacetime list.
    *   *Reference Benchmarks:* Cite **Dora Farms (Case 06)** and **Chinese Embassy (Case 01)** to prove that peacetime targeting errors are a result of systemic planning negligence.
*   **5.3 Violation of the Principle of Proportionality:** Prove that launching a cruise missile at a target immediately adjacent to an active school carries a predictable, disproportionate risk of civilian harm.
    *   *Reference Benchmarks:* Cite **Katyr-Yurt (Case 03)** and **Mastaba Market (Case 18)**.
*   **5.4 Command Confirmation Bias:** Reconstruct how analysts misidentified the school as part of the base due to remote visual assumptions.
    *   *Reference Benchmarks:* Cite **Kabul Drone Strike (Case 05)**.

### SECTION 6: JURISDICTIONAL LINK & EUROPEAN PUBLIC PROSECUTION
*   **6.1 Domicile of the Complaining Entity:** Detail PFPJ ry's registration in Helsinki, Finland (Business ID: 3616815-5).
*   **6.2 Universal Jurisdiction under Finnish Criminal Code:** Detail Chapter 11 of the Finnish Criminal Code, which permits prosecution of war crimes regardless of where they occurred.
*   **6.3 Procedural Obligation of European States:** Argue that European authorities have a binding duty to investigate international war crimes under their human rights obligations.
    *   *Reference Benchmarks:* Cite **Hanan v. Germany (Case 02)**.

### SECTION 7: PROSECUTORIAL RECOMMENDATIONS & REMEDIES
*   **7.1 To the Finnish General Prosecutor:** Formal request to open a criminal investigation against the targeting planners.
*   **7.2 To the ICC Office of the Prosecutor:** Request to incorporate the Minab factual dossier into active Article 15 communications.
*   **7.3 Evidentiary Preservation:** Annex the verified names and profiles of the 168 children as a permanent historical record.
