## 📌 Executive Summary of the Case

* **The Incident:** On February 28, 2026 (9 Esfand 1404), precision-guided BGM-109 Tomahawk cruise missiles struck the **Shajareh Tayyebeh Elementary School** in Minab, Iran (`27°08'43"N 57°05'02"E`).
* **The Toll:** **156 certified martyrs** (including **120 elementary pupils aged 6–12**, **26 female educators & staff**, 7 parents, 2 community workers, and 1 unborn infant) and **95+ documented injured**.
* **The Forensic Core:**
  1. **Munitions Debris:** High-resolution recovered booster and wing-actuator serial numbers confirm US Navy BGM-109 Tomahawk land-attack missiles.
  2. **10-Year Satellite Chronology (2013–2026):** Proves the school compound was separated by a concrete barrier wall from an adjacent military facility back in 2016 and operated continuously for a decade as a civilian primary school.
  3. **Violation of Precaution (IHL):** Under Article 57 of Additional Protocol I to the Geneva Conventions, relying on outdated mapping data without real-time visual confirmation constitutes severe command negligence.

## 📋 Thematic Discussion Guide & Questions for Sama

### Section 1: The Evidence & Fact-Finding (25 min)
* *Key Question:* "How do open-source intelligence (OSINT), satellite archival tracking, and physical debris analysis transform civilian casualty verification in modern warfare?"
* *Focus:* The transition from unverified media fog to a legally robust, judicial-standard indictment of 156 certified victims.

### Section 2: Command Negligence & the Duty of Precaution (25 min)
* *Key Question:* "When military planners claim a 'mapping error' or outdated intelligence, what does International Humanitarian Law (Article 57, Additional Protocol I) require regarding real-time verification before striking?"
* *Focus:* Is an intelligence lapse a legitimate legal defense, or does it establish culpable negligence and war crime liability?

### Section 3: Universal Jurisdiction & European Legal Pathways (25 min)
* *Key Question:* "What realistic legal and parliamentary pathways exist within Finland (Chapter 1, Section 7 of the Criminal Code) and the ICC (Article 15 communication) to pursue accountability for educational attacks?"
* *Focus:* Concrete next steps for legal filings, victim compensation mechanisms, and Nordic diplomatic engagement.

### Section 4: Audience Q&A and Closing Synthesis (15 min)
* Sama synthesizes the key insights and introduces PFPJ ry’s formal Article 15 petition call-to-action.
