## 1. Asra / Asma Zakeri — resolved

**Decision (project lead, 2026-11-15):** Asma is the same child as Asra Zakeri — the two spellings
were a transcription variant (اسما / اسرا). Asra is the older sister. Her correct portrait is
`asra-zakeri.jpg` (`r01_c07`, roster 91). The `r02_c07` photograph is **not** Asra.

**Why the data agreed:** the v3 registry has 120 students with roster numbers 1–120, no gaps and no
Asma; the only name the extraction stage produced that v3 dropped was Asma (`r02_c07`); the older
generator had split that one family slot into two children with conflicting ages/grades (Asma age 8
Grade 2, Asra age 7 Grade 1); the v3 Zakeri cluster is complete at 8 named members.

## 2. Adrina Pegah — only student without a portrait

- Registry summary: 119 studio portraits for 120 students; her record
  (`Campaigns/minab/data/minab_incident_dataset.json`, around line 2597; `memorial-data.js` line 2753)
  has `photo_url`, `photo_path` and `photo_grid` all `null`.
- Every boy has a portrait; she is the only student in that state.

  (`PFP_Platform/web/public/js/reconstruction/scene/memorial-gallery.js`, line 1018), so the girls'
  hall builds 46 frames under a banner that now says 47.
- When the portrait arrives: place it in `PFP_Platform/web/public/images/children/`, set `photo_path`
  on her `DATASET_V3` record and re-run the generator. The frame appears automatically.
- Alternative if no portrait should be shown: a nameplate-only placeholder frame for records
  without a photo.

## 9. Bot dataset is pre-v3

`PFP_Platform/bot/data/memorial-data.json` (loaded by `PFP_Platform/bot/services/memorial_service.py`)
is a flat 120-record list after the Asma removal. It does not match v3:

- 14 of its ids do not exist among the v3 students (some are teachers, e.g. `fereshteh-sangarzadeh`,
  `samira-basardeh`, `roghayeh-karimi`; some are old slugs such as `mahdiyeh-zakerikhah`).
- 14 v3 students are missing from it.
- Its ids and fields (`name`, `motherName`, `photoGrid`) come from a different generation.

Regenerate it from `DATASET_V3` (or point the bot at `children.json`) before relying on bot search
results.
