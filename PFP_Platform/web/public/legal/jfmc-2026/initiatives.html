Global Initiatives & Synergy Directory

Comprehensive directory of organizations, campaigns, and global activities supporting the Minab Children incident · Curated for the Board of Trustees to drive coalition and synergy.

Stars of Minab (Setareh-haye Minab)

Youth & Art International Coalition YouTube: @StarsOfMinab
High Synergy

An international youth-led coalition and campaign dedicated to preserving the memory of the elementary schoolchildren killed in the strike. The organization utilizes narrative art and media to represent the children as stars whose light continues to reach the world.

Core Activities

  • Organizing the international exhibition \"Children of Minab Still Draw the Sun\" featuring recovered children's artwork and tributes.
  • Publishing digital memory projects and detailed testimonial videos on YouTube.
  • Creating public awareness around the \"permanent absence\" of the children's futures.

Synergy Action Plan

Memorial Linking: Cross-link PFP's digital memorial mosaic directly with their platform. Coordinate with their artist network to produce digital assets and high-impact memorial card graphics for volunteer campaigns.

168+1 Campaign

Legal & Advocacy Diaspora Initiative Hashtag: #من_صدای_مینابم
High Synergy

An international media and legal campaign established in memory of the 168 victims of the Shajareh Tayyebeh school strike. The campaign is structured around building public pressure and legal cases to hold target states accountable.

Core Activities

  • Coordinating international legal actions and petitions demanding formal investigations.
  • Building a comprehensive digital archive of victim testimonies and media reports.
  • Mobilizing international public opinion through targeted social media hashtags.

Synergy Action Plan

OSINT Sharing: Establish a channel to share PFP's forensic data (weapons mapping, satellite coordinates) with their legal analysts. Co-schedule social media pressure actions (e.g., `#OneMonthMinab` anniversary event).

Forensic Architecture

Legal & Advocacy Research Agency London / Global
Medium Synergy

An academic-led research agency that uses spatial and visual mapping to investigate state violence and violations of international law.

Core Activities

  • Developing advanced 3D spatial reconstructions of the airstrike site at Shajareh Tayyebeh Primary School.
  • Analyzing weapon debris, entry trajectories, and blast radius maps to identify military weapon systems.

Synergy Action Plan

Visual Evidence: Embed their 3D structural analysis within PFP's public-facing Evidence Portal (`evidence.html`) and incorporate their findings into Universal Jurisdiction legal filings in Germany.

Human Rights Activists in Iran (HRA)

Legal & Advocacy NGO Established Peer
Medium Synergy

An independent human rights organization focused on monitoring and documenting violations of human rights in Iran.

Core Activities

  • Gathering on-the-ground testimonials, hospital data, and victim profiles.
  • Organizing letters and joint statements demanding DoD accountability and policy audits.

Synergy Action Plan

Data Cross-Verification: Cross-reference PFP's database of 100 verified child victims with HRA's verified lists to achieve absolute dataset integrity before submitting documents to the UN.

Amnesty International & Human Rights Watch

Legal & Advocacy Global NGOs Established Authority
Medium Synergy

Leading international human rights NGOs dedicated to documenting war crimes, advocating for victims, and auditing civilian harm mitigation policies of major military powers.

Core Activities

  • Publishing detailed investigation briefs declaring the Minab school strike a potential war crime.
  • Engaging U.S. defense officials directly to demand systemic investigation guidelines.

Synergy Action Plan

Resource Distribution: Integrate their official investigation publications into PFP's public Amplify portal, utilizing their institutional authority to support our volunteer action campaigns.

U.S. Congressional Oversight

UN & Oversight Legislative Inquiry Washington DC
Medium Synergy

Formal inquiry efforts initiated by U.S. lawmakers targeting the Department of Defense regarding the application of AI and targeting errors during the war's outbreak.

Core Activities

  • Formal letter sent by Rep. Yassamin Ansari demanding U.S. DoD transparency on civilian casualty counts.
  • Senate Armed Services Committee action restricting travel funds for the Secretary of Defense until civilian harm reports are released.

Synergy Action Plan

Legislative Briefing: Prepare concise, prosecution-grade briefing dossiers on the Minab school attack and send them to the oversight staffers of Senators Gillibrand, Slotkin, and Rep. Ansari.

UN Human Rights Council (UNHRC)

UN & Oversight Intergovernmental Body Geneva
Low Synergy

The primary intergovernmental body within the UN system responsible for strengthening the promotion and protection of human rights globally.

Core Activities

  • Conducted an urgent debate on the protection of education and children in armed conflict.
  • Special Rapporteur reports addressing the strike, alongside resolutions condemning military actions against school infrastructure.

Synergy Action Plan

UN Special Procedures: Submit formal evidence packets to the Special Rapporteur on the Right to Education and the Special Rapporteur on Human Rights in Iran to keep the case prominent on the UN agenda.

UNICEF Protest Artists & Grassroots Tributes

Youth & Art Grassroots Protest Tehran, Iran
Low Synergy

Local artists and victims' families who staged peaceful memorials and installations in Iran, utilizing physical art to voice grief and demand international action.

Core Activities

  • Constructing a symbolic mock destroyed classroom installation in front of the Tehran UNICEF office.
  • Displaying drawings and personal items of the deceased children to humanize the tragedy.

Synergy Action Plan

Digital Archiving: Create a dedicated gallery space on PFP's public portal showcasing digital replicas or photos of the classroom installation and children's drawings to convey the human cost of the strike.

Strategic Governance Notice

⚠️ Independence & Neutrality: While certain commemorative initiatives are backed or launched by official Iranian government channels (such as the Ministry of Cultural Heritage plans for a permanent secretariat, museum, and state-sponsored mourning processes), People for Peace & Justice ry (PFPJ ry) must maintain a strict distance. To secure international legal standing, universal jurisdiction support, and observer status at the Assembly of States Parties (ASP), the association must remain strictly independent, apolitical, and neutral.
