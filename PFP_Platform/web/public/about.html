People for Peace & Justice ry

An independent, registered international human rights and legal advocacy association domiciled in Helsinki, Finland.

⚖️ Our Mission & Purpose

People for Peace & Justice ry (PFPJ ry) is established to advance international justice, accountability, and legal recourse for human rights violations and war crimes committed against civilians, with a particular focus on the protection of children in zones of conflict.

Our core activities operate across three main avenues of legal advocacy:

  • Legal Research & Forensic Documentation: Compiling open-source intelligence (OSINT), satellite mappings, weapon shell fragment metrics, and geolocation data that meet the rigorous standards of international tribunals.
  • International Litigation Support: Preparing Article 15 Communications for the Office of the Prosecutor of the International Criminal Court (ICC) and dossiers for Universal Jurisdiction complaints in Europe.
  • Victim Advocacy: Preserving the memory of casualties, establishing permanent digital memorials for victims, and linking affected families with international legal networks.
