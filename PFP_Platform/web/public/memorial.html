The Two Unrecovered Schoolchildren (شهدای جاویدالاثر)

Of the 120 martyred schoolchildren on the official school roster, 118 were identified and buried through visual recognition or DNA matching. Two young boys — 7-year-old 1st-grade gymnast Makan Nasiri Khouzani (born 1397/10/09) and 9-year-old 4th-grader Mohammad-Taha Jafari (born 1395/02/25) — are officially recorded as "Martyred, Remains Unrecovered" (شهید ولی شناسایی نشده) due to the extreme thermal and blast force at the impact point.
