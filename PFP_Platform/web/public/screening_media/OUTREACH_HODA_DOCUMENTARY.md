## 📧 2. English Official Request Letter (For International / Institutional Archives)
**Subject:** Screening Request: "Hoda" Documentary for Minab Memorial & Accountability Event in Helsinki

Dear Mr. Hamed Saadat / DEFC International Department,

I am writing to you on behalf of **People for Peace and Justice ry**, an independent non-governmental association legally registered in Helsinki, Finland (Business ID: 3616815-5).

Our association is hosting a dedicated cultural and human rights event in Helsinki, Finland, focused on international accountability, documentation, and memorialization for the victims and surviving children of the February 28, 2026 Shajareh Tayyebeh school strike in Minab.

We have closely followed the production of the documentary film **"Hoda" (*هدا*)**, directed by Hamed Saadat and produced under the Documentary, Experimental and Animation Film Center (DEFC). The film’s focus on the survival, enduring psychological trauma, and resilient spirit of the school's children represents a crucial testimony that deserves to be witnessed by the international community and human rights advocates in the Nordic region.

We would be deeply honored to request:
1. Authorization for a non-commercial, cultural screening of *"Hoda"* during our Helsinki gathering.
2. A high-resolution screener link (Vimeo / secure download) and English subtitle file (.srt), or permission for our translation team to provide localized English subtitles.
3. If schedules permit, an invitation for the director to join an online Q&A session or send a video message for the Helsinki audience.

Thank you very much for your time, dedication, and solidarity with the children of Minab.

Warm regards,

**Mahdi Farimani**
