Casualty Reconciliation: Resolving 168 vs. 156 Certified Martyrs

Following the Tomahawk missile impact on February 28, 2026, search-and-rescue teams collected approximately 312 fragmented biological samples from the rubble, yielding initial field estimations of 168–180 casualties. Over the subsequent 40 days, the Legal Medicine Organization of Hormozgan (LMO) conducted short tandem repeat (STR) genomic typing against family blood reference samples. Minab Prosecutor Ebrahim Taheri formally certified the final official casualty count of 156 martyrs with legally registered death certificates.
