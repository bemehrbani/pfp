Our Strategy

Three Pillars of Legal Advocacy

A comprehensive legal initiative demanding accountability and international criminal prosecution.

01 🔍

Fact-Finding & Documentation

Compiling verified digital evidence (OSINT), satellite imagery, weapon shell metrics, and survivor testimonies regarding the Minab Incident.

02 ⚖️

Strategic Litigation

Preparing Article 15 Communications to the International Criminal Court (ICC) and formal complaints to European Prosecutor Offices under Universal Jurisdiction.

03 🎓

Expert Vetting

Collaborating with academic councils and legal scholars to validate targeting-error metrics and command negligence precedents.
