Roadmap

Advocacy Roadmap & Milestones

Transparent procedural phases from initial evidence gathering to formal court dockets and international representations.

✓
Q1 2026 (Completed)

Fact-Finding & Memorial

Initiated open-source intelligence verification, satellite analysis, weapon fragments mapping, and established the digital memorial for the 156 victims.

✓
Q2 2026 (Completed)

Entity Registry & Advisory Board

Successfully registered the legal entity PFPJ ry in Helsinki, Finland, and established the Academic Advisory Council with experts in IHL.

●
Q3 2026 (Current Phase)

Expert Vetting & Precedents

Vetting the 10 similarity factors and 18 precedent cases with international law scholars to finalize the forensic validation dossier.

⧖
Q4 2026 (Upcoming)

Universal Jurisdiction Filing

Filing formal complaints with European prosecutors under Universal Jurisdiction and submitting an Article 15 Communication to the ICC.

⧖
2027+ (Future)

Victim Recourse & Expansion

Representing victims in litigation proceedings, advocating for prevention policies, and extending documentation to other civilian incidents.
