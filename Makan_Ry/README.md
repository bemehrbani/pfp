# Makan ry (انجمن ماکان)

**Makan ry** is a registered non-profit, humanitarian, and independent non-governmental association (NGO) founded in Finland. Its mission is to advocate for peace, oppose foreign colonial interventions and military aggression, challenge cruel unilateral sanctions targeting civilians, and foster public awareness and solidarity among Iranians, immigrants, and the Finnish public through trilingual engagement (Persian, Finnish, English).

## 🏛️ Governance & Core Structure

- **Jurisdiction:** Finland (Associations Act / *Yhdistyslaki 503/1989*)
- **Filing Authority:** Finnish Patent and Registration Office (**PRH** - *Patentti- ja rekisterihallitus*)
- **Registration Cost:** €58 (online electronic submission)
- **Minimum Founders:** 3 natural persons
- **Chairperson (*Puheenjohtaja*):** Must be 18+ and resident in Finland
- **Organs:**
  - **General Assembly (*Vuosikokous / Yhdistyksen kokous*):** Supreme authority
  - **Board of Directors (*Hallitus*):** Executive and legal representation (Chairperson + 2–6 members)
  - **Operations Inspector (*Toiminnantarkastaja*):** 1 auditor + 1 deputy auditor for financial and legal inspection
- **Membership Fee:** €5 / month (set by General Assembly)
- **Official Languages of Dissemination:** Persian, Finnish, and English
