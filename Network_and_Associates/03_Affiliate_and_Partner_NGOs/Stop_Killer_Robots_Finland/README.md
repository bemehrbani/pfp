## 2. Direct Case Alignment: Digital Dehumanisation & Automated Target Review in Minab

The **28 February 2026 airstrike on the Shajareh Tayyebeh Girls' Elementary School in Minab** is one of the clearest documented real-world examples of what Stop Killer Robots warns against:
1. **Legacy Database Reliance & Automated Target Validation (`EXH-CENTCOM-004`):**
   - US CENTCOM's preliminary AR 15-6 investigation admitted that targeting officers relied on an outdated pre-2016 DIA database entry and automated target-review pipelines ("circular review") without human verification of 10 years of satellite imagery (`2016–2026`) showing the school's concrete separation walls, painted murals, and sports courts.
2. **Failure of Meaningful Human Control:**
   - Just as in **Case 05 (*Kabul Ahmadi Family Drone Strike, 2021*)** and **Case 15 (*Dahyan School Bus Airstrike, 2018*)** in PFPJ ry's [4-Case Factual Comparison Brief](../../../Campaigns/minab/hxrc_nov20_event_package/MINAB_4_CASE_FACTUAL_COMPARISON_BRIEF.md), automated confirmation bias turned "human-in-the-loop" review into a fatal rubber stamp, killing 156 civilians (+ 1 unborn child).
