**Sibling Repository Path:** `../Makan_Ry/` (relative to `Projects/`)

## 1. Mission & Charter Alignment with PFPJ ry

Makan ry is a non-partisan, independent Finnish–Iranian civil society association dedicated to:
1. **Opposing War, Military Intervention, and Economic Sanctions:** Defending civilian lives, human dignity, and sustainable peace.
2. **Strict Independence:** Zero organizational, financial, or political dependence on any government, embassy, military apparatus, or political party (`transparency_and_ethics.md §1.1`).
3. **Humanitarian & Parliamentary Dialogue in Finland:** Building constructive bridges with Finnish peace organizations (*Rauhanliitto*, *Sadankomitea*, *Lääkärin sosiaalinen vastuu*), researchers, and members of the Finnish Parliament (*Eduskunta*).
