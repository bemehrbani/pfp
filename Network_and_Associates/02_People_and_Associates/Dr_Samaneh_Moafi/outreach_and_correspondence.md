### A. Formal Collaboration Email (14 October 2026)

**To:** `info@forensic-architecture.org`, `am@forensic-architecture.org`  
**Subject:** Collaboration Inquiry (ATTN: Dr. Samaneh Moafi, Isabella Parlamis & Minab Team) — Walkable "Before & After" Immersive Memorial for Minab School (Helsinki, Nov 20)

> Dear Dr. Samaneh Moafi, Isabella, Angela, and the Minab Investigation Team (Nicholas, Elliot, and Jack),
>
> I am writing to you on behalf of **People for Peace & Justice ry (PFPJ ry)**, a registered non-profit human rights and peace association in Helsinki, Finland, and **Pomegroup Studio**.
>
> First, thank you for your July 2026 investigation, **"Airstrikes on Minab Primary School"** (Ref: 121). Seeing how your team reconstructed the Shajareh Tayyebeh School from pre-strike photographs and post-strike drone footage brought vital clarity and dignity to the 120+ schoolchildren and 26 educators killed on February 28, 2026.
>
> ### What We Are Building & How We Hope to Collaborate
>
> On **November 20, 2026 (World Children's Day)**, we are hosting a public memorial and documentary screening in Helsinki dedicated to the children of Minab.
>
> Alongside the documentary screening and our *"100 Faces of Peace"* portrait tribute, we want to create an **immersive, walkable "Before & After" spatial experience** where visitors can walk through the school courtyard and assembly hall while listening to an audio-guided narrative of the children's story:
> 1. **Before the Strike:** Experiencing the vibrant schoolyard, the painted perimeter murals, and the children's daily environment as reconstructed from the school's archive.
> 2. **After the Strike:** Transitioning into the post-strike spatial evidence to understand the physical reality and human cost of the February 28 attack.
>
> We have already built a self-hosted, open-source WebXR prototype for the project (`https://peopleforpeace.live/reconstruction.html`), and we have an established creative technology footprint in Helsinki (including past immersive exhibitions with the Helsinki XR Center).
>
> **We would love to ask for your help and collaboration in two ways:**
> 1. **3D Asset / Exhibition Loan:** Would Forensic Architecture be open to sharing or licensing the 3D architectural mesh / camera-aligned assets from Investigation 121 (`.blend`, `.obj`, or `.glb`) and high-resolution video segments so we can build this walkable audio-guided installation faithfully around your verified spatial work?
> 2. **Methodological Guidance or Brief Input:** If Samaneh, Nicholas, Elliot, or Jack have 15 minutes for a quick call, we would be honored to receive your guidance on translating your spatial findings into an ethical, walkable before-and-after audio experience for the public.
>
> Our event is strictly non-commercial and educational, with full credit and prominent attribution to Forensic Architecture and your investigation team.
>
> Would you be open to a brief 15-minute call next week to explore this?
>
> With deep respect and gratitude,
>
