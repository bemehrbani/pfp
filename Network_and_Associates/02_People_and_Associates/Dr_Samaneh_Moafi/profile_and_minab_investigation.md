## 1. Academic & Professional Background

- **Forensic Architecture (Since 2015):** Provides conceptual and methodological oversight across Forensic Architecture's investigations. Oversaw the *Centre for Contemporary Nature* (2019–2022), led *The Grenfell Tower Fire: Situated Testimonies* (2018–2024 — an interactive 3D spatial walkthrough combined with survivor audio testimonies), and contributed to *A Cartography of Genocide: Israel's Conduct in Gaza Since October 2023* (2023–2024).
- **Doctoral Research:** Completed her PhD thesis titled *Home Rebellion*, exploring shared practices of spatial resistance, state-initiated housing, and gender and class relations in Iran.
- **Architectural Teaching & Practice:** Practiced architecture in Australia; taught BA Architecture at the University of Technology Sydney (UTS), MArch Urban Design at the Bartlett School of Architecture (University College London — UCL), and led courses at the Royal College of Art (RCA) and the Architectural Association (AA).
